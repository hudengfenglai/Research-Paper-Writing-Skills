# Venue adaptation

Venue policies change. Treat page limits, required sections, anonymity rules, disclosure forms, and supplementary-material rules as variables to verify from the current official call for papers.

## Adaptation card

```text
Venue and year:
Official CFP URL:
Main-page limit:
Reference and appendix policy:
Required statements or checklists:
Anonymity and citation constraints:
Submission format and deadline:
Verified on:
```

## What can be adapted safely

- Section order and page allocation
- Abstract length and terminology
- Figure and table density
- Required reproducibility, ethics, limitation, or broader-impact statements
- Review rubric and rebuttal format

## What must remain evidence-bound

Venue style does not justify stronger novelty language, omitted limitations, unverified citations, or unsupported numbers. When a format constraint removes detail, move the detail to an allowed supplement or state the remaining limitation.
