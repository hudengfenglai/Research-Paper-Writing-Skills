# Related Work writing guide

Related Work positions the paper so that a reviewer can verify the gap and the distinction.

## Workflow

1. List the closest competing papers and the baselines used in the experiments.
2. Group the literature by technical idea, assumption, or failure mode.
3. For each group, summarize the common mechanism and its relevant limitation.
4. End with the exact distinction supported by the paper's method or evidence.

## Paragraph pattern

`topic scope -> representative mechanisms -> limitation relevant to this paper -> technical distinction`

Prefer a small set of representative, verifiable citations over a long citation dump. Cite the strongest relevant work even when it makes the paper's improvement smaller. Keep historical context, current-paper claims, and speculation in separate sentences.
