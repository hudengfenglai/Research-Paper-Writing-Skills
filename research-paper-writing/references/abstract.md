# Abstract writing guide

The abstract is a compressed claim-evidenceevidence argument. Draft it after the story card and claim-evidenceevidence matrix exist.

## Structure

1. State the task and setting.
2. Name the exact technical challenge or unresolved gap.
3. State the contribution or insight and the mechanism that implements it.
4. Report the strongest verified evidence with its comparison context.
5. State the scope boundary or practical implication when space permits.

## Checks

- A reader can identify what was done and why it matters in one pass.
- Every number has a source anchor and names the comparison when needed.
- The abstract does not claim causality, generalization, or superiority beyond the matrix.
- Method names are introduced without unexplained terminology.
- Generic field-level openings are removed when they do not support the paper's story.
